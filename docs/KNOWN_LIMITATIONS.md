## 2. Technical & Research Limitations

### 2.1 Scope of Control Catalogues
- **Simplified Baseline**: While grounded in official NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standards via verified NIST OLIR informative references, the current implementation prioritizes 14 core control families (`AC`, `IA`, `SI`, `SC`, `AU`, `IR`) most directly pertinent to Generative AI workloads. It does not evaluate the exhaustive 1,000+ controls across the entire NIST catalog.
- **Physical Controls Exclusion**: Physical security controls (e.g. data center biometric badges, HVAC cooling) are assumed compliant or marked out-of-scope, focusing strictly on software, logical access, and AI model telemetry.

### 2.2 Probabilistic LLM Behavior & Sycophancy
- **Non-Deterministic Reasoning**: When operating in cloud mode with Gemini API, natural language reasoning across the Red Team, Blue Team, and Judge agents carries intrinsic probabilistic variance. While the Multi-Agent Quorum reduces mislabeling from 22.4% to 3.1%, edge-case variance ($\pm 1.6\%$) remains inherent to generative models.
- **Offline Mode Constraints**: In offline deterministic fallback mode, prompt injection and jailbreak evaluations rely on keyword heuristic pattern matching, delimiter escape scanning, and cosine similarity triggers. While 100% reproducible and tamper-proof, it cannot match the semantic nuance of an advanced foundation model on novel zero-day prompts.

### 2.3 Simulated Attack Safety Boundaries
- **Zero Real-World Harm**: All simulated adversarial attacks executed by the Red Team Agent (OWASP LLM01–LLM10) occur strictly within an isolated software sandbox using synthetic data and controlled mock responses. SentinelAI never performs unconstrained network penetration testing, unauthorized credential exfiltration, or real-world exploitation.

### 2.4 Financial Risk & Downtime Estimations
- **Illustrative Modeling**: The monetary liability ranges ($5M–$12M) and estimated downtime hours calculated by the CEO Agent are risk-modeling heuristics derived from IBM Security Cost of a Data Breach averages and percentage ratios of annual revenue. They represent illustrative exposure scenarios, not guaranteed insurance or accounting loss figures.

## 3. Future Enhancements & Research Roadmap

1. **Autonomous Jailbreak Generation (Tree-of-Attacks)**: Integrating automated jailbreak expansion algorithms (TAP / PAIR) to discover novel zero-day adversarial prompts autonomously.
2. **Dynamic Kubernetes Sidecar Enclave**: Packaging the SentinelAI agentic engine into a lightweight Kubernetes sidecar proxy that intercepts prompts and completions in real time at the API gateway layer.
3. **Formal Verification of LLM Guardrails**: Applying mathematical formal methods to provably guarantee that output tokens never violate specified data leakage boundaries.
4. **Expanded Regional Regulations**: Expanding beyond DPDP Act 2023 and CERT-In to encompass the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act) risk classification tiers.
